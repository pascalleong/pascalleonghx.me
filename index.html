The System

Your degree bundles 4 assets at one price.

The 4 Hidden Assets: the 4 separable assets bundled into every degree at a single price: Credential, Knowledge, Status, and Infrastructure. Each is extracted by exactly one pillar of Degree Arbitrage.

Credential

Extracted by Brand Arbitrage

Borrowing the trust of your university's name, or anything that carries it (a club, a title, an event), so people take you seriously before you have your own proof.

Knowledge

Extracted by Academic Arbitrage

Optimizing your unit and curriculum choices to maximize the hard skills and proof of work the market pays for, not the grades the rubric rewards.

Status

Extracted by Identity Arbitrage

Using your low-credentialed identity to access high-status networks. Getting in the room with decision-makers drastically increases the likelihood of landing your Dream Outcome.

Infrastructure

Extracted by Resource Arbitrage

Using what your university gives you access to (money, facilities, software, clubs, events, speakers, the alumni network) for your own goals, at little or no extra cost.

Who's behind this

So I started extracting maximum value from all 4. Grades stayed, as one asset out of 4 instead of the whole game. For me, it meant direct contact with founders and C-suite executives, and a shorter path to internships and graduate roles of my own. Every founder I met is one more door I can open for the students I work with.
