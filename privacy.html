Privacy policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

What I collect

  • Your email, when you sign up for the free tools or the newsletter. The sign-up goes to Substack, which sends the emails.
  • Your answers, when you apply for the 1-on-1. The form runs on Tally, and I keep the answers in Notion to run the programme.
  • How you use this site: pages you open, buttons you click, your rough location and your device. Google Analytics collects this with cookies.
  • Anything you send me yourself, by email or by message.

Why I collect it

To send you what you asked for, to run the 1-on-1, and to see which pages help students so I can make them better.

Who else sees it

Substack, Tally, Notion and Google store parts of this data for me, under their own privacy policies. I do not sell your data to anyone.

Your choices

  • Every email has an unsubscribe link.
  • You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings.
  • You can ask me to delete your data. I delete it within 7 days of your request, and I tell you when it is done.
